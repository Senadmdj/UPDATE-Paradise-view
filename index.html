WHO IS PARADISE VIEW FOR?

A place for every
kind of traveller.

Whether you’re travelling close to home or coming from across the world, Yangzhou invites you to discover it at your own pace.

Paradise View welcomes domestic Chinese visitors, international travellers, couples, families, photographers and culture lovers seeking a memorable escape. From peaceful scenery and traditional gardens to regional food and cultural discovery, the campaign encourages visitors to explore thoughtfully and support local dining, accommodation, guides and tourism businesses along the way.
